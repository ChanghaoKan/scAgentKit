For this versioned request, wrap the otherwise unchanged complete annotation.v1 proposal in an outer object with exactly schema="scagentkit.annotation-draft.v1", proposal, risk_flags. risk_flags is an array with exactly one record for every cluster.
Each risk record has schema="risk_flags.v1", stage="annotation", cluster_id, and fields (an array of five records). Each field has exactly name, value, assessment_status, source="model", evidence_ids (an array).
Required names are doublet_risk, batch_status, qc_celltype_concern, cycle_concern, ambient_suspected. General risk values: not_assessed, not_detected, suspected. Batch values: not_applicable, insufficient_evidence, fully_confounded, no_effect_detected, effect_detected_design_supported.
assessment_status is assessed, not_assessed, or not_applicable. Unassessed general risks use not_assessed; unassessed batch uses insufficient_evidence. Not-applicable general risks use not_assessed; not-applicable batch uses not_applicable. Assessed records need evidence_ids such as pool:GENE from that cluster's supplied markers.
These flags are bounded interpretations for review, not biological proof. Do not claim that absent batch, doublet, cycle, QC or contamination measurements were assessed. Keep not_assessed when the supplied evidence is insufficient. Do not omit fields.
