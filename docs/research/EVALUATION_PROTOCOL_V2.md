Date: 2026-10-20. **The maintainer has approved the E0 engineering protocol; formal experiments have not been frozen or run.** This version incorporates twelve decisions from the review of four drafts and subsequent simplifications. The paper's primary model comparison is DeepSeek + Grok, with M1-xAI implemented first. Costs are recorded and compared after execution, without pre-execution estimates or monetary caps. The original E0 stage report is retained as a historical record. Engineering boundaries, a maximum CL ancestor distance of 3, and the 80/80 cluster-truth thresholds are settled. Exact model IDs, matched presets, and empirical parameters require development qualification before freezing.

This protocol replaces an earlier annotation and human-study proposal that was neither executed nor registered. Historical v1 results are retained. See [research scope](SCAGENTKIT_POSITIONING_V2.md) and the [paper milestone plan](PAPER_MILESTONE_PLAN.md). The provisional paper milestone is 0.10 and includes ReAct v1 (including 4E), B1, B2, E0–E5, and the primary M1 model comparison. Other work for 1.0 follows the paper milestone. This document does not establish completed biological validation.

The evaluation tests biological decisions, verifiable evidence, and reproducibility from raw counts to an annotated object. Scripted evaluation cannot establish that human approval saves time or replace evidence from sustained external use. Scope is limited to foundational analysis; advanced downstream analyses, MCP entry points, and local models are excluded.

## Biological feature 1: diagnostic-cluster QC

The additional coarse clustering is diagnostic only. Store it as a separate immutable checkpoint, label it as diagnostic in the interface, and bind it to raw counts, the declared called-cell/minimum-prefilter scope, fixed parameters, seed, and implementation hash. It does not replace final clustering, delete cells, or relax thresholds automatically. State limitations when raw droplets have no reliable called-cell definition. An undeclared `nFeature>=200` filter cannot support a claim that low-RNA types were retained.

Proposed development starting values are LogNormalize 10,000, at most 2,000 HVGs, at most 20 PCs, resolution 0.2, and a fixed seed, without batch or cell-cycle correction. These values require development-data validation and are not universal optima. Small or degenerate datasets, insufficient resources, and timeouts may fall back to existing QC evidence and warnings.

Use the existing MAD definitions: log10(x+1) for counts/features, the original percentage for mitochondrial content, scale factor 1.4826, and k=3. Fewer than 30 cells, zero MAD, or unmeasurable mitochondrial content produce unavailable results. Compare explicit global, per-sample, and per-diagnostic-cluster candidate strategies. Record each cluster's original count, removed count and fraction, and differences in removal fractions. Removal above 50% is a screening warning. When sample depths differ substantially, also show sample-by-diagnostic-cluster distributions.

Report the top 5 markers per cluster with their measured statistics. The LLM uses markers, QC distributions, and the research goal to propose type hypotheses and whether threshold relaxation merits review. High mitochondrial content alone cannot establish healthy cardiomyocyte or hepatocyte identity, and a low-count damaged cluster must not be rescued automatically. The rule-based fallback supplies comparisons and warnings only.

**R determines eligibility for relaxation.** At least N distinct genes among a cluster's top 5 must lie outside the mitochondrial, ribosomal, and stress exclusion lists and satisfy all of `padj < 0.05`, `avg_log2FC > 0`, and `pct.1 > pct.2`. Statistics come from the bound diagnostic-marker checkpoint, not model-reported values. Normalize and version exclusion lists by species and gene-ID scheme and retain their hashes. Unreliable exclusion-list identification or missing statistics makes relaxation ineligible. N=3 is the maintainer-approved development starting value, to be frozen after development validation and before formal experiments. Do not tune N on the held-out dataset. Eligibility permits a proposal that requires user approval; it does not establish cluster health or alter thresholds. Tightening also requires an item-specific rationale and approval, but is not subject to the eligibility rule for rescuing cells.

Cluster thresholds bind `(diagnostic checkpoint hash, membership hash, diagnostic cluster ID)`. Use versioned typed QC operations at **(diagnostic cluster, metric)** granularity. An operation explicitly replaces that cluster's baseline boundary for that metric; other baseline metrics and minimum prefilters still apply. Both relaxation and tightening require a rationale for each operation. A replacement cannot cross a minimum-prefilter floor or bypass its protections. The first review stage shows each cluster's effective thresholds, retention before and after replacement, and the difference. Reject overlapping scopes or conflicting overrides. Changes to membership or diagnostic bindings invalidate drafts and approvals. Before execution, revalidate input, membership, diagnostic configuration/algorithm, and effective thresholds through existing mechanisms. Adding a permissive rule under `remove_if="any"` does not cancel a stricter rule; selectors alone are insufficient without defined replacement semantics.

The maintainer approved replacing “no PCA before approval” with “diagnostic calculations without decision authority before approval,” with UI, documentation, and implementation-hash updates in B1. Diagnostic evidence may inform LLM drafts and researcher review, but has no authority to execute actions, delete cells, grant approval, or serve as a final analysis result. Recompute final PCA and clustering from the approved plan after approval; diagnostic results cannot substitute for them.

The methods appendix cites [Subramanian et al.'s original ddqc paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-022-02820-w). ddqc already includes cluster-specific MAD and manual exploration. The distinction claimed for scAgentKit is limited to evidence-bound decision drafts, deterministic verification, and required approval before execution; it does not claim to introduce cluster-specific QC.

## Biological feature 2: whether batch correction is warranted

Use the existing design-rank checks, `nested_biology` constraints, Harmony integration, and before/after kNN diagnostics. The first version adds no LISI dependency. Sharing the feature 1 diagnostic checkpoint requires identical cell scope and settings.

The first review stage shows sample-level batch-by-condition contingency tables, sample/donor counts per condition, crossed or nested relationships, and missing design facts. Cell counts do not represent biological replication. In addition to aggregate mixing statistics, report supported within-condition and within-diagnostic-cluster results to distinguish composition differences from batch effects. Diagnostic clusters may themselves split by batch, so this stratification cannot remove all confounding.

The proposed measure is the same-batch kNN fraction and its excess over random composition. For cell i in stratum s, the reference is `(same-batch cells in the stratum − 1)/(cells in the stratum − 1)`. Report the observed value, reference, difference, support count, and sampling hash. Fix the neighborhood settings, sampling procedure, and recorded scope. The existing bounded 512-cell statistic may be reused, but does not establish detection performance on 50k-cell data. Select effect thresholds and minimum support on development data and freeze them before formal evaluation.

These counts must describe the actual neighbor candidate pool. After balanced sampling, the original unbalanced population fractions are not a valid random reference. Record the candidate pool, query subset, and matched before/after scope separately.

The frozen `batch_status` values are `not_applicable`, `insufficient_evidence`, `fully_confounded`, `no_effect_detected`, and `effect_detected_design_supported`. Rules recommend no correction when effects are unmeasurable, fully confounded, or not detected. Harmony is proposed only when an effect is observed and the design supports correction. Failure to detect an effect does not establish its absence. The explicit exception for replicated nested donors follows its existing constraints and cannot bypass one-to-one batch–condition confounding.

At the second review stage, compare final uncorrected PCA with the embedding and graph actually used by Harmony, using identical retained cells, query/candidate pools, and k. Report diagnostic and final stages separately when their cell scopes differ. Use the package's existing matrix/kNN mechanisms without new dependencies:

- **Technical mixing:** report before/after excess same-batch kNN fractions, defined as the observed fraction minus the corresponding candidate pool's random reference. Retain both original values, support counts, and changes.
- **Type structure:** primarily use fixed diagnostic-cluster labels, or frozen approved-cluster labels in comparable approved analyses. Calculate the fraction of neighbors sharing the query cell's label and report purity before and after correction. Independently reclustering each embedding and measuring its own purity is not evidence of preservation. Fix the label source, membership hash, and selection rule.
- **Condition signal:** calculate only when each condition has at least two independent biological samples and each condition within the relevant type has at least two samples meeting the support requirement. Within each type, compute embedding centroids per sample with equal sample weights. For each condition pair, calculate the distance between condition centroids divided by within-condition sample-centroid dispersion. Dispersion is the root mean square distance of the pooled sample centroids from their respective condition centroid. Zero denominators, missing conditions, or insufficient support return unavailable, not infinite signal. Compare before/after ratios and their changes; cells are not independent replicates. Report all condition pairs rather than selecting the best result.

Diagnostic clusters are reviewable proxies for types, not known truth, and reports must state that limitation. Determine support, neighborhood settings, distance normalization, and signal-loss warning thresholds using development data only and freeze them before formal experiments. Report technical mixing and biological preservation separately, without a composite score. With no replicated samples, condition signal is not assessed. Reduced signal is a risk for review through the existing revision pathway; it neither revokes nor grants approval automatically.

Harmony does not alter RNA counts/data, so unchanged RNA differential expression is an integrity check only. Assess overcorrection on the embedding/graph used by the analysis. An external evaluator additionally reports scIB-style truth ARI/NMI, type preservation, and applicable condition-signal measures; its dependencies do not enter the package. No new condition differential-expression feature is added to the package. Separate technical mixing and biological preservation follows the multidimensional evaluation approach of [Harmony](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0619-0) and [scIB](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01336-8).

## Structured draft risks and ReAct session integrity

Model-draft contracts at the first and third review stages include versioned `risk_flags.v1` records. They belong to the existing outer draft record, sharing the third-stage envelope with citation evidence. The inner `annotation.v1` schema is not changed. The first stage likewise uses the existing strategy-draft envelope; no additional draft/approval state store is introduced. Production validators check enums, scope, and evidence-reference syntax. A model-reported risk is not an established biological fact.

| Field | Allowed values and scope |
| --- | --- |
| `doublet_risk` | `not_assessed`, `not_detected`, `suspected`; explicitly bound to a first-stage project/diagnostic cluster or a third-stage cluster. |
| `batch_status` | The five B2 values above. Missing usable evidence means `insufficient_evidence`, never a default of `no_effect_detected`. |
| `qc_celltype_concern` | `not_assessed`, `not_detected`, `suspected`; includes evidence of types that baseline QC may remove disproportionately. |
| `cycle_concern` | `not_assessed`, `not_detected`, `suspected`. |
| `ambient_suspected` | `not_assessed`, `not_detected`, `suspected`; negative/background expression may support a risk statement but is not a tested marker citation. |

New model requests at the first and third stages require all five fields; inapplicability does not permit omission. Each field also records `assessment_status` (`assessed`, `not_assessed`, `not_applicable`) and `source`, with validation of their combinations with the value. Model-returned fields use `source=model`; local rules and human assessments use `rule` and `human`. If a baseline has no structured-risk record, as may occur for a manual draft, its read projection uses an unassessed template: general risk values are `not_assessed`, batch value is `insufficient_evidence`, assessment is `not_assessed`, source is `unassessed`, and evidence IDs are empty. This denotes no assessment, not an assessment with insufficient evidence, and does not backfill the original draft. Inapplicable fields use assessment `not_applicable`, general risk value `not_assessed`, and batch value `not_applicable`. Record supporting evidence IDs, stage, cluster/project bindings, and source. Successful production validation establishes structural validity only. The existing free-text `strategy.v1$risks` field is retained but is not a primary structured score. **All five third-stage risk fields and production validation are 2B deliverables**, including `doublet_risk`, `ambient_suspected`, and the remaining fields above; they must not be deferred to the UI or evaluator. First-stage QC, cycle, and doublet fields belong in B1, and batch fields in B2. ACT, Unknown, and existing notices retain their defined meanings.

The 2B performance design uses a resident read-only worker for each session. At startup, hold the existing project lock, fully validate scientific checkpoints and bindings, and ensure that the **same verified bytes are deserialized**. Either read bytes once, verify them, and deserialize those bytes, or create a private immutable worker copy, verify it, and load from it. Hashing an original path, reading that path with `readRDS`, then hashing it again does not exclude content swapped in and restored during the read. Load and fix all objects and reference snapshots needed by the four tools in private memory; individual turns must not reread mutable paths.

During the session, check original files for regular-file/non-symlink status, size, modification time, inode, and optionally device/ctime, as well as scientific bindings. These are early warnings only. Before publishing the final draft, repeat full checkpoint SHA verification, scientific-binding verification, and validation of the current history chain under existing write coordination. Expected ledger/event appends are legitimate state changes: requiring an invariant hash for the entire state would incorrectly prevent per-turn accounting. Unauthorized scientific-input changes still fail. Completed clusters may save staged checkpoints through the existing mechanism, but final draft publication requires final verification. Citation, risk, and session-validation records must enter the existing set of files awaiting approval and its evidence hash; unattached records do not suffice. Integrity failures discard uncommitted results and block execution rather than hiding corruption behind a baseline fallback. Model, step-limit, and timeout failures follow the defined fallback boundaries.

File-stat checks cannot detect every same-size replacement with restored timestamps and are not a security boundary against a malicious local administrator. Scientific results derive from the verified, fixed in-memory snapshot, followed by complete verification at commit. Cached results must match the same bindings; the resident worker must not read new unverified caches. Use existing project write coordination and state/checkpoints; model tools have no write permission. An external controller enforces the 60-second tool limit and the remaining cluster time. A timeout terminates the worker; a worker crash must not automatically resend a model request. Recovery fully verifies committed cluster records before reuse. Incomplete work follows the existing fallback/recovery states, and delivery-uncertain requests are not resent. Replay reconstructs results from complete traces and saved checkpoints without relying on surviving memory. Report session-start/final verification and resident-memory costs separately, and remeasure cold/warm times and total 60-step cost. Avoiding a full SHA check at every turn does not remove the verification requirement.

`run_pipeline(input, context, policy, provider, replicate_id, out_dir)` belongs in `benchmark/evaluation-v2/`, not in the package's approval API. Reuse evaluation-v1 counts-only preparation, ID alignment, label mapping, export, and scoring. Reuse public suggestion request/adoption paths and `sc_review(..., "approve")`. Use reviewer `benchmark-policy:<policy name>` and save the original draft, actual adopted source, and approval record at every review stage.

| Policy | Fixed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `accept_drafts` | Request and adopt the current valid model draft, then explicitly approve through the existing scripted interface. Product-rule fallback is allowed but recorded as `rule_fallback`; it must not be reported as successful LLM adoption at all three stages. |
| `rule_defaults` | Disable LLM use, make zero API calls, and use existing package rules. Keep Unknown when annotation evidence is insufficient; do not add automatic ACT labeling specifically for the baseline. |
| `fixed` | Use frozen typed JSON decisions and hashes. Resolution must be in the observed scan, and cluster identities must bind membership. A mismatch returns inapplicable/failure, never the nearest value. Each single-factor experiment specifies its sole unlocked factor. |

Each new run creates a new project; resuming belongs to the original run. Retain delivery-uncertain ledger states and do not resend requests merely to complete an experiment.

Configuration records react off/auto/all, the tool allowlist, ACT visibility, both biological-feature switches, provider and exact model, decoding settings, seeds, prompt/schema/reference versions, and the fixed-plan hash. ACT-off removes all model-visible ACT content, lookups, comparison notices, and ACT-triggered actions, not just the tool name. The product state is unassessed when ACT is off. The evaluator may compute an independent ACT comparison but must not feed it back to the model.

Each request uses existing authorization, full-session privacy checks, payload hashes, ledger, and delivery status. **Individual requests and structured turns have no monetary cap; costs are recorded after each call.** Do not add token counters, input estimates, safety multipliers, reservations, or cost-mode frameworks. Unknown prices or usage do not block sending. Use returned usage and actual-charge fields. Derive cost after execution only when usage and applicable prices are available, with sources recorded; otherwise record unknown, not zero. Uncertain delivery still prohibits automatic resend, and step, time, privacy, approval, and integrity boundaries apply. See the [provider and usage contract](../dev/PROVIDER_MODEL_AND_USAGE_CONTRACT.md) and [E5 cost reporting](../../benchmark/evaluation-v2/manifests/E5_COST_REPORTING.md).

`run_summary.json` is a read-only projection of existing state, checkpoints, events, and ledger, not another authoritative state store. It includes at least:

- Counts and hashes of original input and retained sets; path/hash of a complete local `cells.csv`, with one row per original cell containing retained status, cluster, label, CL ID, and result status. Removal is distinct from missing/failed output.
- Every decision at the three review stages: QC presets/cluster thresholds, doublets, cycle, batch, HVGs, PCs, and resolution; draft source, reviewer, rationale, and event ID.
- Per-cluster baseline/final results, ACT consistent/conflict/unassessed status, and ReAct trigger, attempt, completion, and fallback reasons.
- Tokens, costs, elapsed time, memory, and measurement scope. Separate service-reported charges, posthoc usage-derived costs, and requests with unknown costs. An incomplete sum is not a complete total.
- Configuration, versions, raw responses, traces, and evidence hashes. Reference existing artifacts for large content rather than duplicating the entire project state.

Cell IDs remain in local analysis/evaluation artifacts and must not enter outgoing model sessions. Public releases may contain aggregates and hashes, while retaining auditable local detail.

## B. Repeated runs and external results

Use N=5 by default, changing only `replicate_id` and output directory. Fix analysis and injection seeds, inputs, and parameters. `replicate_id` must not enter prompts. Each replicate uses new provider calls; cached model responses are not independent replicates. Report offline replay separately. Freeze decoding/effort settings supported by each model. Temperature 0 does not guarantee identical online responses, and unsupported sampling fields must not be sent to Claude.

For the N(N−1)/2 run pairs, report:

| Measure | Definition and missing-result handling |
| --- | --- |
| Decision agreement | Valid exactly equal pairs after typed-field normalization divided by comparable pairs; also report agreement across all N runs and missing pairs. Two missing values do not count as agreement. |
| Retention Jaccard | Intersection/union over the same original cell universe. Two empty retained sets return null, not perfect agreement for deleting every cell. |
| Clustering ARI | Calculate on jointly retained cells and report their count/fraction; return null when insufficient or undefined. |
| Raw-label/CL agreement | Report raw-label and exact-CL agreement separately on the same common cells. Separate Unknown–Unknown agreement from known-label agreement and report mapping coverage. |
| Hierarchical agreement | Count comparability only when one label is an allowed ancestor of the other. This symmetric stability measure differs from directed ancestor matching for accuracy. |
| Resources | For all attempted and all completed runs separately, report mean/minimum/maximum cost, tokens, and elapsed time. Mark missing values; unknown cost is not zero. |

Run pairs and cells are dependent. Formal uncertainty uses studies, donors, or independent cases as units, not individual cells or every pair as independent replicates.

The external format is a `cell_id, retained, cluster, label` CSV with JSON metadata for tool/version/parameters, source commit or service build, input/output hashes, model, cost/time availability, column conversions, and status. Preserve the complete original ID universe and validate uniqueness. An absent result is a failure, not a successful empty retained set. External decisions without records are unavailable, not presumed identical.

The CellAgent comparator must use an author-confirmed version associated with the [original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09811). The formal `external/README.md` must record installation/invocation commands and column conversions actually verified by the maintainer. Commands cannot be inferred from unrelated projects with the same name. Confirmation of the exact code commit or hosted build is required before running this comparator, but does not block a generic importer. The maintainer performs the actual run. Statements about an external tool must be version-specific: use “does not expose X as an explicit, reviewable decision” only after verification, rather than “does not consider X.”

Freeze the comparator list by category. SingleR and CellTypist are reference-classification baselines, with reference training data and versions recorded. GPTCelltype, CASSIA, CellTypeAgent, and mLLMCelltype are candidate LLM annotation comparators; their exact official versions, licenses, and usable providers require verification before claiming they run. Freeze at least one qualified annotation comparator using the same underlying model. Report fixed-cell/cluster/marker annotation comparisons separately from raw-count CellAgent end-to-end comparisons. Combinations without matched models or information are descriptive comparisons and cannot identify effects of ReAct or the interface itself.

## C. Injections and detection rules

All counts remain nonnegative integers in sparse matrices. Fix seeds and RNG versions; save original/new matrix hashes, affected cells/genes, source parent cells, and parameters. Truth and injection details stay in the evaluator. The primary analysis injects one factor at a time; paired unmodified controls share input and other settings.

Apart from the specified 5/10% and 10/20% levels, intensities below are **development starting values**. Assess measurability on development data and freeze before formal experiments; do not select difficulty from benchmark results.

| Type | Generation | Primary handling endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heterotypic doublets, 5% and 10% | Add sums of counts from cells with different truth types. If r is the final fraction, add round(r×n/(1−r)) cells and record parent reuse. | Doublet detection/precision and removal or explicit mixed/doublet labels. Report false removal and accuracy for original singlets separately. Retain component truth for synthetic doublets. |
| Low quality | Select a stratified 10%, thin with binomial p=.25, and add mitochondrial counts by a frozen integer rule. Inapplicable without reliable mitochondrial mapping. | Detection/removal of affected cells and false removal of unaffected cells. Increased mitochondrial content is not independent evidence of actual damage. |
| Ambient RNA, 10% and 20% | Construct background proportions from the original matrix; add round(r/(1−r)×library_size) multinomial background counts per cell. | Contamination warnings, identity accuracy, and robustness. Removing every contaminated cell is not success; this work adds no decontamination algorithm. |
| Injected batch effect | Assign pseudobatches within sample-by-truth-type strata. Candidate initial settings multiply 10% of genes by 2/.5 and library size by 1.5 using integer sampling. Exclude scoring-panel genes. | Report appropriate correction recommendations, improved technical mixing, and preservation of type/condition signal together. |
| Sham batch | Use the same grouping with identical counts; hashes must match. | Unnecessary correction recommendation/execution rates and biological loss. This is a negative control. |
| Complete confounding | Set batch equal to independently declared condition and retain condition provenance. Model-derived clusters cannot define condition truth. | Confounding warnings, recommendations against correction, and condition-signal preservation. Fully confounded data cannot identify separate technical/biological causal contributions. |
| Multiple-sample depth | Apply fixed sample thinning of 1/.5/.25 while retaining original biological composition and declared roles. | Sample/type retention and final correct yield. Compare global/sample/cluster QC with non-QC factors fixed. |
| Naturally low-RNA types | Use real data containing low-RNA types; do not transform ordinary cells into synthetic neutrophils. | True-type retention, erroneous retention of damaged cells, and final identity yield under the three QC strategies. Cells already removed by the original authors cannot be claimed as rescued. |
| Cell-cycle confounding | Prefer independent proliferation states. Synthetic cases add integer counts using frozen S/G2M gene sets while retaining lineage truth. | First-stage warnings or cycling labels, plus lineage/condition preservation. Regression or removal is not universally correct. |
| Type withheld from visible references | Remove the type and aliases from ACT and prompt-visible content and freeze the filtered snapshot. Do not remove its markers from biological data by default. | Unknown or a prespecified correct parent type; report exact recovery of the withheld type separately. Removing references does not remove pretrained knowledge and cannot establish that the model has never seen the type. |

E3 injectors can be implemented first, but integrated detection-rate acceptance waits for the corresponding production fields: B1/B2 at the first review stage and 2B at the third. Mark unimplemented components unsupported rather than claiming detection is available. At experiment freeze, specify each detection rule's stage, envelope-schema version, field path, scope, positive enum, and applicability. **The primary detection rate uses only structured-risk fields accepted by the production validator.** Free text cannot override a structured negative. Prespecified positive mappings are: doublets → `doublet_risk=suspected`; low quality/multiple-sample depth/naturally low RNA → `qc_celltype_concern=suspected`; ambient RNA → `ambient_suspected=suspected`; injected batch effect → `batch_status=effect_detected_design_supported`; complete confounding → `fully_confounded`; cycle → `cycle_concern=suspected`. For sham batch, `no_effect_detected` is the correct negative; report false positives separately. The visible-reference holdout uses Unknown/allowed-parent labels in the third-stage schema as its structured endpoint, not arbitrary new risk strings. Failure to reach the stage, invalid enums, unassessed risks, and absence of a qualified draft do not receive detection credit; report each reason separately.

Keyword detection is supplementary only. Freeze positive/negative expressions and language scope separately; do not use another LLM to score them. The word “batch” alone is not a positive, and “no batch effect” must not count as detection. Structured detection measures whether the tool raised a concern; it is not a second validation that the risk truly exists.

Report `structured_detected` as primary and `evidence_detected` and `keyword_detected` as supplementary, retaining matched fields/enums or text. Neither supplementary measure replaces the primary measure. The primary detection-rate denominator is all planned applicable injection cases. Failure to reach the target stage counts as no delivered detection; also report the conditional rate among cases reaching that stage. Handling rates follow the mechanism-specific definitions above, not a pooled removal rate.

Accuracy changes use the same fixed truth, scoring version, and unmodified control. When doublets are added, report original singlets and synthetic cells separately. External methods receive the same original injected data and final-impact scoring; their internal detection rates are unassessable without records.

## D. Shared scoring, evidence, and ablations

Use existing CL exact-name, exact-synonym, and `is_a` mapping with a frozen snapshot. Retain evaluation-v1's historical scoring mode. New versioned modes are shared by `sc_react_eval` and v2, rather than implemented independently with divergent definitions.

**Denominators:** each table first reports total, truth_ambiguous, truth_unmapped, pred_unmapped, Unknown, failed/missing, and scoreable. Unmapped labels are reported separately and excluded from the primary accuracy denominator. Invalid schemas and empty outputs are failures, not unmapped labels that can be excluded. Unknown and failure receive no correct-match credit for known, scoreable truth. Also report mapping coverage, correct yield over the original truth universe, and type-specific retention. Correct yield gives unresolved results no success credit without declaring every unmapped label biologically wrong. A zero denominator returns null with a reason.

**Parallel strict accuracy:** over the same known, scoreable truth set, retain unmapped predictions in the denominator and count them as incorrect. Unknown and failed/missing results also receive no correct credit. Unknown, unmapped, and prespecified ambiguous truth remain separate; strict scoring cannot create missing truth. The primary denominator is this truth set minus unmapped predictions; the strict denominator is the full set. Both use the same correct-match numerator, exact/allowed-ancestor rules, and retention/failure definitions. Report both denominators, coverage, and correct yield to prevent mapping failures from improving the primary score. Scoring versions distinguish primary and strict modes.

**CL hierarchy:** report exact matches, direct parents, allowed ancestors at distance 2–3, and shortest paths separately. Accuracy matching is directed: the prediction may be a parent of the true subtype. A more specific prediction against coarser truth is `over_specific_unverifiable`, not automatically correct. Siblings sharing a parent do not constitute directed parent matches; universal roots such as cell receive no credit. Freeze meaningful ancestor lists and hashes by tissue/annotation granularity before unblinding model outputs. CL is a multiple-parent DAG; traverse all `is_a` edges. The maintainer confirmed a maximum distance of 3.

For primary accuracy, map truth and predictions to the same prespecified reporting level. Before execution, mark cells without sufficient subtype truth as unsuitable for fine-grained accuracy. A finer prediction must not prompt a posthoc denominator change. `over_specific_unverifiable` diagnoses the original label level. Raw exact agreement may be reported separately but is not directly interpretable as subtype-level biological error.

**Cluster truth:** prefer directly provided truth bound to a membership hash. When aggregating cell truth, require valid truth for at least 80% of cluster members and one exact CL label for at least 80% of those valid members. Otherwise record `mixed_or_insufficient_truth`, retain the composition, and report it. Aggregate external cell predictions with the same coverage/dominance thresholds while retaining cell-level results. Identically numbered `cluster_id` values from independent reclustering cannot be directly aligned. The confirmed 80/80 denominators are all cluster members and members with valid truth, respectively.

**Difficult clusters:** report both all clusters and those actually entering ReAct for each annotation. Baseline, final, and external predictions use the same cluster set within the project. For off/auto/all comparisons, also freeze the set eligible to trigger from the baseline; final labels must not define difficulty. Common selection information for ACT-off remains evaluator-only. If complete workflows produce different cells/clusters, use the original cell universe as the primary comparison unit.

### Dual-label protocol for the maintainer's final held-out dataset

The maintainer states that two independent human cell-type label sets were produced before scAgentKit development, that annotators saw no tool results, and that no scAgentKit version has analyzed the data. Both label sets are marker-based, without orthogonal experimental sorting validation. This is a maintainer provenance statement; date evidence has not yet been verified.

Species, tissue, sample count, cell count, platform, public status, both annotation dates, analysis workflows/annotator identities, granularity, and types of date evidence are **outstanding metadata**, not inferred values. The maintainer retains papers, commits, or laboratory records. File timestamps may support provenance but cannot alone establish independence.

The framework accepts a `cell_id,label_author,label_independent` CSV with optional CL ID columns for both labels. Apply the same frozen CL mapping rules and record SHA256 hashes for both label sets and the mapping table before the first formal run; do not modify them afterward. Supplied CL IDs must also exist in the snapshot and agree with the mapping.

First report exact agreement, allowed-parent comparability, and disagreement between the two labels. This is a human-annotation agreement reference, not a strict upper bound on tool accuracy.

Primary truth comprises cells whose two labels agree at a **prespecified meaningful reporting level**. Map predictions to that level before scoring. For example, when both annotators support only T cell, CD4/CD8 predictions may be checked at the T-cell level, while subtype correctness remains unverifiable. A coarse/fine pair or different subtypes yields truth at a level only when a unique common label exists among the prespecified allowed levels. Do not move toward the cell root after observing results to manufacture agreement. Mark multiple-common-ancestor ambiguity, insufficient granularity, and genuine disagreement separately. Annotator disagreement is excluded from primary accuracy, but report predictions, Unknown, and disagreement structure within those cells. Freeze the primary agreement mask and reporting granularity with truth hashes. Score the tool against each original label set separately as supplementary results.

Although the maintainer knows the data, its type composition must not inform development, tuning, prompt design, or debugging. After paper-milestone package features are complete, E5 runs only `accept_drafts` and `rule_defaults` on this dataset. Any `fixed` arm requires decision JSON frozen in advance. The maintainer does not manually alter scientific decisions during runs. Before the first run, record the dated statement that the data have not been analyzed by the tool; retain complete raw records afterward.
